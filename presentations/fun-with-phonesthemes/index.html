Summary

Fun with Phonesthemes introduces recurring sound patterns that can suggest related meanings in English words. The lesson calls these patterns phonesthemes and demonstrates them with several initial consonant clusters.

The examples connect cl- with closing or tight contact, gl- with light or vision, sn- with the nose or related actions, sl- with messy motion, and fl- with light or small movement. The final point encourages learners to notice patterns alongside vocabulary and grammar.

Important points

  • A phonestheme is a recurring sound pattern associated with related meanings.
  • The relationship is a tendency, not a grammatical rule.
  • Cl- appears in words associated with closing, holding, gripping, or contact.
  • Gl- appears in words associated with light, vision, or shining.
  • Sn- appears in words associated with the nose or related actions.
  • Sl- appears in words associated with sliding, slipping, slime, slush, or messiness.
  • Fl- appears in words associated with light or small movements.
  • Recognizing sound patterns can help learners notice connections among words.
