Intermediate 08 A Bad Connection

← Back to VideosWatch on YouTube ↗

Transcript

Jerry Rogers works for Allied Shipping. His company handles a lot of international shipments for Brian Gordon’s firm, TMA. One day, Jerry called with some important news for Brian. Brian’s personal assistant, Kathy, answered.

Trying to reach Brian

Jerry: Hello. I’d like to speak to Brian Gordon, please.

Kathy: May I ask who’s calling?

Jerry: Yes. This is Jerry Rogers at Allied Shipping.

Kathy: Could you please speak up a little? I can’t hear you very clearly.

Jerry: It’s Jerry Rogers calling from Allied Shipping.

Kathy: Please hold, Mr. Rogers. I’ll see if Mr. Gordon’s available.

Jerry: Thank you.

Kathy: (a few seconds later) I’m sorry, Mr. Rogers. Mr. Gordon is on the other line. May I ask what it is in reference to? Maybe I can help you.

Jerry: I’m calling about a shipment of cosmetics that’s scheduled to leave for Milan, Italy…

Kathy: Oh, Mr. Gordon is free now. I’ll put you through.

Jerry: Thank you.

A bad connection

Brian: Hello, Jerry. What’s up?

Jerry: Hi, Brian. Listen, I may have a problem meeting the delivery schedule we agreed on for that shipment to Milan. It looks like it’ll be delayed at least a month.

Brian: I’m sorry, Jerry. I didn’t catch that. What did you say?

Jerry: I said we are having problems with the shipment to Italy, and…

Brian: What?… Jerry? We have a terrible connection. I can barely hear you.

Jerry: I’m calling from inside the warehouse. I don’t have much of a signal on my cell. Only one bar.

Brian: Go outside and let me call you back, Jerry. At this number, right?

Jerry: Yes. 656-287-8663.

Brian: 8663. Okay. I’ll call you right back.

Calling back

(They both hang up, and Brian calls Jerry back.)

Jerry: Hello, Brian?

Brian: Oh, that’s much better. Now, what were you saying?

Jerry: I was calling to let you know that the shipment going to Italy will be delayed at least a month.

Brian: What?!… I think it was better when I couldn’t hear what you were saying!
