Intermediate 02 You’re Going the Wrong Way

Transcript

Looking for the Applegate Building

Jack Hampton has been in New York on business for several days. It’s 8:45, and he has a 9:00 appointment. He’s just gotten off the bus in Midtown Manhattan and is trying to find the Applegate Building.

Jack: (to himself) Well, where is it? I knew I should have taken a cab. (to a passerby) Excuse me… sir?

Passerby: Yes?

Jack: Could you tell me how to get to the Applegate Building? It’s supposed to be on this street somewhere, but I can’t seem to find it.

Passerby: Sorry, I’m from out of town myself. There’s a police officer over there. Try him.

Jack: Thanks.

Asking the police officer

Jack: (to the police officer) Excuse me, Officer? I’m trying to locate the Applegate Building. It’s supposed to be on 55th Street, but I don’t see it anywhere.

Officer: Do you have the number?

Jack: Yes, let’s see. (searching through his pockets) I should have it here somewhere, if I haven’t lost it. Oh, here it is. (He pulls a piece of paper out of his pocket.) It says 15 East 55th Street.

Officer: You’re headed the wrong way. You’re on West 55th Street.

Jack: Just my luck! How do I get to East 55th?

Officer: Turn around and go back to 5th Avenue. It’s only two blocks. That’s where West changes to East.

Jack: You mean I’m on the right street, but going in the wrong direction?

Officer: Right. Number 15 will be in the first block on the right-hand side of the street.

Finding the subway

Jack: Oh, one more thing! Can you tell me where the nearest subway is? I have to get to the Freedom Tower by 2:00.

Officer: Sure. When you leave the Applegate Building, go right to 3rd Avenue—that’s four blocks—then turn right again and go two blocks south to 53rd Street. You’ll see a subway station on the right. You can’t miss it. Take the E train downtown.

Jack: Okay. Let’s see if I’ve got this straight—right out of the building to 3rd Avenue, then another right to 53rd Street. Got it! Thanks very much!

Officer: No problem. Take it easy!
