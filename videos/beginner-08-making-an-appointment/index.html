Transcript

The pain

Oh, Peter. You don’t look well.

I don’t feel well.

What’s wrong?

I have a pain in my arm.

Are you taking anything for the pain?

Just aspirin.

When did the pain start?

Let’s see. What’s today’s date?

It’s November 29th.

Did the pain start today?

It started on September 21st, when I got back from Italy. Now it is getting worse. I am going to make an appointment with a traumatologist.

Tell them exactly what is wrong when you make the appointment. Why don’t you call them now?

Yes, I will. I have the number on my phone.

The wrong number

Hello?

Hello. Is this Dr. Martin’s office?

No, it’s not. I think you have the wrong number. Are you a zombie?

Ah, no. Of course not.

What is your name?

My name is Peter.

What do you do for a living?

I’m a psychologist.

What?

A psychologist. I help people with their problems. Um, what do you do for a living?

I’m a zombie. I eat brains for a living.

Oh, my God. I am going to hang up now.

Nice talking to you. You have a delicious voice.

Oh. Okay. Goodbye.

Goodbye.

Making the appointment

Did you call the doctor?

I tried. I got a wrong number.

Who did you call?

You really don’t want to know.

Well, try again. Maybe you misdialed.

Hello. Dr. Martin’s office. This is Beatrice. How may I help you?

Thank goodness. I don’t want to speak to any more zombies.

Zombies?

Forget it. I’d like to speak to Dr. Martin, please. Is he there?

He’s with a patient. Maybe I can help you. What is the problem?

My arm. It hurts when I move it.

Would you like to make an appointment?

Yes, as soon as possible.

What is your name, please?

Peter Mangiaracina.

The doctor is free on Wednesday at 9:00 a.m.

Wednesday is going to be a busy day for me. Can we make it Thursday, around 10:00 a.m.?

The doctor is free at 10:15 on Thursday.

That sounds good.

Okay. Thursday at 10:15.

I’ll be there.

Have a nice day.

You too.

A sudden recovery

So, did you finally talk to the doctor?

No, but I spoke to the receptionist, and I made an appointment for Thursday morning.

Very good. I’m sure he will give you something to make your arm feel better.

Well, about that…

What? What are you doing?

My arm. I can move it again.

Really?

Yes. Now I have to call and cancel the appointment.

That’s not a problem, is it?

No, not unless a zombie answers the phone again.
